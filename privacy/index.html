Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

This policy covers the RENOVO iPhone app and the client progress tracker at fastaminoswellness.com/tracker (together, "the tracker"). Both are run by Pete Garza, doing business as RENOVO COACH and FAST AMINOS WELLNESS ("we", "us"). The app and the website tracker use the same account and the same data.

What we collect

  • Account details your coach enters: your name, email, sex, date of birth, height and goal.
  • What you log: check-ins (weight, tape measurements, body fat readings), progress photos, notes, how you're feeling (energy, hunger, sleep), GLP-1 side effects if your coach turns that on, and the workouts you log (exercises, weights, reps).
  • Apple Health data, only if you connect it in the app: daily steps, sleep, body weight, active calories, exercise minutes and resting heart rate. You choose exactly which of these to share in Apple's permission screen, and you can change that anytime in the Health app.
  • Your coach's plans and replies to your check-ins.

We don't use advertising trackers or analytics in the app or the tracker.

How we use it

Only to coach you: to show your progress, build and adjust your plan, and reply to your check-ins. Your information is visible to you and your coach.

Apple Health data

  • Apple Health data is used only to show your progress to you and your coach in the tracker.
  • It is never sold, never used for advertising or marketing, and never shared with data brokers or other third parties, except the hosting provider below that stores it for us.
  • The app only reads from Apple Health. It never writes to or changes your Health data.

Where it's stored

Your data is stored with Supabase, our secure database and sign-in provider, and is sent over encrypted connections. Database rules make sure each client can only see their own records. Progress photos are kept private and shown only through links that expire.

What we never do

  • We don't sell your personal information.
  • We don't share it with other companies for their marketing.
  • We don't use it to make automated decisions about you.

Your choices

  • Disconnect Apple Health anytime: Health app → your profile picture → Apps → RENOVO.
  • Turn reminders off in your iPhone's Settings → Notifications → RENOVO.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, a correction, or deletion of your account and everything in it. Email info@renovocoach.com. We'll confirm within 30 days. Deleting your account removes your check-ins, photos, workouts, plans and Apple Health records.

Health information

The tracker is for fitness coaching and education. It is not medical care and doesn't diagnose or treat any condition. If you have severe or lasting symptoms, including GLP-1 side effects, contact your prescriber or a healthcare professional.

Children

The tracker is for adults 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. Important changes will be shown in the app.
