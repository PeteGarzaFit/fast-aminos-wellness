Help for the RENOVO Fit iPhone app

Need help? Email info@renovocoach.com and we'll get back to you, usually within 1–2 business days.

My numbers aren't showing

  • Open Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices › RENOVO Fit and make sure every category is turned on.
  • Readiness, HRV, resting heart rate and sleep stages need an Apple Watch worn during the day and to bed.
  • Pull down on any tab to refresh.

My 6-month charts look empty

When you first allowed Health access, iOS may have asked how much history to share. If you chose "Past 30 days", change it in Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices › RENOVO Fit.

Where does the food data come from?

RENOVO Fit reads calories, macros and water that your food-tracking app (for example MyFitnessPal, Cronometer or Lose It!) saves to Apple Health. Turn on Apple Health sharing in that app's settings.

Is my data private?

Yes. RENOVO Fit has no account and no servers; your health data never leaves your iPhone. See the privacy policy.
