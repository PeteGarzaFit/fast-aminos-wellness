Workout

Back

Better posture and a stronger pull.

ChestBackShouldersArmsLegsGlutesCoreFull Body
Exercises
5
Total sets
17
Time
45–55 min

The workout

Warm up first: 5 minutes of easy cardio, then 1–2 light sets of the first exercise.

  1. Pull-Up or Lat Pulldown

    4 sets6–10 repsRest 2 min

    Start each rep by pulling your shoulder blades down. Drive elbows toward your back pockets.

  2. Barbell or Dumbbell Row

    4 sets8–10 repsRest 2 min

    Hinge to about 45° with a flat back. Row to your lower ribs and pause for a beat.

  3. Seated Cable Row

    3 sets10–12 repsRest 90 sec

    Sit tall and don't rock. Let your shoulders stretch forward, then squeeze them back together.

  4. Single-Arm Dumbbell Row

    3 sets10–12 per armRest 60 sec

    Brace on a bench and pull the dumbbell toward your hip, not straight up to your armpit.

  5. Face Pull

    3 sets15 repsRest 60 sec

    Rope at face height. Pull toward your eyes and finish with your hands beside your ears.

Pick the right weight

Choose a weight you can lift for the listed reps with 1–2 good reps left in the tank.

Progress each week

When you hit the top of the rep range on every set, add a little weight next time.

Protect your muscle

Especially while dieting or on a GLP-1: lift 2–4 days a week and hit your daily protein target.

Know your protein number?

Training builds the signal; protein supplies the material. Get your daily calories and protein target in about 2 minutes.
