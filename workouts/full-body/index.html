The workout

Warm up first: 5 minutes of easy cardio, then 1–2 light sets of the first exercise.

  1. Goblet or Back Squat

    3 sets8–10 repsRest 2 min

    Brace your core, sit between your hips, and drive up through the whole foot.

  2. Dumbbell Bench Press

    3 sets8–10 repsRest 90 sec

    Shoulder blades back and down. Lower with control to chest level.

  3. Lat Pulldown or Pull-Up

    3 sets8–10 repsRest 90 sec

    Pull your shoulder blades down first, then bring the bar to your upper chest.

  4. Romanian Deadlift

    3 sets8–10 repsRest 2 min

    Hips back, flat back, feel the hamstrings stretch.

  5. Seated Dumbbell Shoulder Press

    3 sets10 repsRest 90 sec

    Ribs down and core tight. Press up without arching.

  6. Farmer's Carry

    3 sets40 yardsRest 60 sec

    Heavy dumbbells, tall posture, short quick steps. Grip and core work in one.

Pick the right weight

Choose a weight you can lift for the listed reps with 1–2 good reps left in the tank.

Progress each week

When you hit the top of the rep range on every set, add a little weight next time.

Protect your muscle

Especially while dieting or on a GLP-1: train 2–4 days a week and hit your daily protein target.

Know your protein number?

Training builds the signal; protein supplies the material. Get your daily calories and protein target in about 2 minutes.
