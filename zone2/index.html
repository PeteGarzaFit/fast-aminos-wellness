The talk test

In Zone 2 you can speak in full sentences, but you couldn't sing. If you can only get out a few words, slow down.

What it looks like

A brisk walk, incline treadmill, easy bike or elliptical. Steady effort you could hold for an hour.

When to do it

Right after lifting, for 30 minutes. That's the 45/30 rule: 45 minutes of strength, then 30 minutes of Zone 2.
